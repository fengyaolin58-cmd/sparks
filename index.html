About

Sparks is a story-focused 2D platformer puzzle, centered around heroes and villains alike in a musically themed world built around song. Heroes and villains have powers too — our main character, Sparks, wields fire-based abilities.

Development

Development is happening in stages, starting with Act 1 — we're working on it hard and will update you as we go until release. If (and only if) we secure enough funding, our co-developers and I will pursue Act 2 and Act 3 to finish the game.

Music

Act 1 will have 7 songs, with hopes to expand the act further since it's a fairly small one. Acts 2 and 3 haven't been developed yet, so their song counts aren't public — though a few have already been written down.

We're a team of three developers, so updates will be few and far between — thanks for your patience while we work hard with each other, and with our beta testers, to build a great experience for everyone.
